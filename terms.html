YAVIR Private Backup is a personal utility controlled by its account owner. It is not offered as a public service and does not sell storage or subscriptions.

Use depends on the owner's existing Google account and storage allowance, a working computer, recoverable encryption credentials, and the independent terms and licences of Google, restic and rclone.

The owner chooses the backup scope and is responsible for preserving the recovery key, checking completion and periodically testing restores. A successful backup of selected files does not imply that every file on the computer is protected.
