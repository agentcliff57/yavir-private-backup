Privacy policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026.

YAVIR Private Backup is operated by its Google account owner for personal backups.

Access and use

The utility requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. It uses that permission to create, list, upload, read and restore its own backup repository. The software can manage files covered by this permission; its configured background backup workflow does not automatically delete backup snapshots. It checks available Drive storage before backups.

Storage and protection

Selected local files and their backup metadata are encrypted by restic on the owner's computer before being sent to the owner's Google Drive. Google can still see storage usage and encrypted object metadata. Google sign-in credentials are kept in the owner's macOS Keychain. A recovery key is kept separately by the owner for restoration. This website receives no backup contents or credentials.

Sharing and retention

The utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or send it to AI models. Google hosts the encrypted repository under the account owner's existing service. Backups remain until the owner chooses to remove them through a separate authorized process. This page has no analytics, cookies or forms added by the utility; its hosting provider may maintain ordinary connection logs.

Control and contact

The account owner can stop the local backup jobs and revoke the connection in their Google Account permissions. Revoking access does not itself delete backups. The support contact is the user-support address displayed by Google on the connection's consent screen.

The utility's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
